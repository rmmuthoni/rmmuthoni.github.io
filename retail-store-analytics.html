Retail Store Analytics

dbt, Snowflake & Power BI

An ELT workflow that loads retail data into Snowflake, transforms it through documented dbt models, and publishes analytics-ready data for Power BI.

View project source

Architecture and design

Source data is loaded before transformation. dbt manages the warehouse models and their dependencies; Power BI reads from the curated gold layer for its semantic model and reports.

Bronze

Retains source-shaped store, customer, product, sales, and returns data.

Silver

Standardizes fields and types, removes duplicates, and validates relationships.

Gold

Organizes analytics models and reporting aggregates for downstream analysis.

Project objectives

  • Consolidate retail data in a cloud data warehouse.
  • Preserve source-shaped data with traceable, repeatable transformations.
  • Standardize data quality and business definitions across reporting.
  • Deliver documented, tested models for business analysis.
